Checks & assumptions

    Road distances and times from the free OSRM routing service (OpenStreetMap data). Router times run about 20% under posted limits, so they are multiplied by the calibration (default 0.80) to get posted-limit time. Your average speed then scales every leg, keeping interstates faster than towns. Speeds above the posted-limit average are flagged in red. Map tiles © Esri, data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
